The signature ritual

The Stackly Hour

Ninety minutes, three stages, one chair. Our most booked appointment- scroll to walk through it.

01 / Arrive

A slow ten minutes

Warm towel, scalp reading, and a walnut-wood tray of jasmine tea while we plan the hour ahead.

02 / Shape

Cut & colour

Dry-cut precision on wet-cut structure, then a botanical gloss matched to your undertone.

03 / Finish

Scalp massage

Twelve minutes of pressure-point massage before the final blow-out and mirror reveal.

04 / Leave

Steadier than you came

A take-home vial of your custom gloss, and a standing invitation back in six weeks.
