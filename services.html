How it runs

Four stages,
every single visit.

Arrive and sit down

Ten unhurried minutes before anything happens- tea, your hair's recent history, and what the week ahead asks of you.

Read and agree

We assess scalp, porosity or skin condition out loud, then quote the full price including any length surcharge. Nothing starts before you say yes.

The work itself

One stylist stays with you start to finish. No chair-hopping, no handing you to an assistant halfway through the colour.

Leave with a plan

A styling walkthrough, the two products that matter for your hair or skin, and the date we'd book you back - written down, not upsold.
